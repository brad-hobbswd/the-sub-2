# The Substitute | Bradley Hobbs

A continuation of the Christian fiction project The Substitute, exploring further themes and story developments from the series.

## Live Website
https://brad-hobbswd.github.io/the-sub-2/

## Overview
The Substitute explores what is happening inside the heart of the person who needs the substitute through discussions of wounds, shame, fear, loneliness, recovery, healing, and the hope found in Jesus.
